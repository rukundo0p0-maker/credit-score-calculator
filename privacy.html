Privacy & analytics

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Credit Score Hub is an independently developed, educational website at yourcreditscorecalculator.com. This notice explains how its calculator, scenario tools and optional analytics work.

Information you enter into the calculators

Credit card limits, balances, payments, loan details, collection information and estimated credit scores are processed in your browser. We do not request a Social Security number, full name, account credentials or a credit report. We do not deliberately transmit calculator entries or score results to Google Analytics.

The calculators do not require a user account or save your financial entries to our website servers. Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure and third-party providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver the pages or support your browsing activity.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-3SHJEG2YQ7) to understand aggregate page traffic, such as pages visited, approximate location, referring website, device/browser information, and general site engagement. Google Analytics may set cookies or similar identifiers and process technical data, subject to Google's policies.

Google Analytics does not load until you choose “Accept analytics.” If you decline or have not made a choice, our site does not load the Analytics tag. We do not send custom credit balances, loan data, collections or estimates as analytics events.

Your choice

Analytics is optional. You can use the full calculator and scenario analyzer whether you accept or decline. Your decision is stored in your browser's local storage so that the site remembers it across pages. You may revisit it at any time using the button below or the preference link in the footer. If you withdraw consent, the page reloads to stop further Analytics activity.

Other sites and service providers

This website is published using GitHub Pages. Clicking educational links (for example, links to myFICO or government websites) takes you to those third-party services, which have their own privacy policies. Learn about Google's information practices at Google Privacy Policy.

Updates

This privacy notice may be updated as features, analytics practices or requirements change. Please review it periodically. We recommend that the website operator obtain jurisdiction-specific privacy advice as the site's audience grows.
