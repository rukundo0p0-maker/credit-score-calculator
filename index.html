Use the amounts currently reported to the bureaus, which may differ from today's online balances. Enter what you plan to pay on each card.

02. Collection accounts

Choose whether each collection remains unpaid, is paid in full, or is settled with a reported $0 balance. Third-party and first-party collections are different.

In FICO 10/10T, paid or $0-settled third-party collections are disregarded. That does not guarantee a score increase. The original creditor's separate negative history may remain.

Methodology & sources. The newer FICO Score 10 suite disregards third-party collections reported paid in full or settled to $0; it also disregards third-party collections with an original amount under $100. Paid medical collections and medical collections under $500 are generally no longer reported by the three nationwide credit bureaus. FICO's collections guidance ↗ · 10 vs. 10T ↗
Footnote: how older FICO versions differ

FICO Score 9 also disregards paid third-party collections, while FICO Score 8 can still consider some paid collections. FICO Score 8, 9 and 10 ignore third-party collections originally under $100. The lender, credit bureau, specific model, and reporting details affect the real outcome; not every lender uses FICO 10 or 10T.

Frequently asked questions

A little more clarity.

Helpful things to know before using any credit calculator.

Does FICO 10T use past balance trends?

Yes. FICO Score 10T can consider at least 24 months of reported history. A current balance or a brief trend description cannot replicate its full inputs. The numbers here remain an independent educational illustration.

Can paying one collection help my score?

FICO Score 10 and 10T ignore third-party collections reported as paid or settled to zero. First-party collections and separate late-payment histories can still matter. Depending on the rest of your file, your actual score could rise, fall, or remain unchanged.

Is this my real FICO® Score?

No. FICO's complete algorithms are proprietary. This site uses publicly published factor weights and independently designed heuristics to create an educational illustration. Only an authorized score provider can report your actual FICO Score for a specific bureau and version.

How is credit utilization calculated?

Total revolving balances divided by total revolving credit limits, expressed as a percentage. For example, $1,000 owed against $10,000 in card limits is 10% utilization. Real scoring also reviews individual accounts and other amounts owed, which this simplified estimator omits.

Does checking the calculator hurt my credit?

No. This website never contacts a credit bureau or requests a credit report. Your answers are processed locally in your browser.

Do salary, age, or job title affect my FICO Score?

These are not included in the standard FICO credit scoring factors. Lenders may evaluate some financial information separately when making a lending decision.

Where can I see my real credit report?

You can access credit reports from the three nationwide credit reporting companies at AnnualCreditReport.com. Credit reports themselves are different from credit scores.
