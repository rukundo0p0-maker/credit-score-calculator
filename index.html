Frequently asked questions

A little more clarity.

Helpful things to know before using any credit calculator.

Is this my real FICO® Score?

No. FICO's complete algorithms are proprietary. This site uses publicly published factor weights and independently designed heuristics to create an educational illustration. Only an authorized score provider can report your actual FICO Score for a specific bureau and version.

How is credit utilization calculated?

Total revolving balances divided by total revolving credit limits, expressed as a percentage. For example, $1,000 owed against $10,000 in card limits is 10% utilization. Real scoring also reviews individual accounts and other amounts owed, which this simplified estimator omits.

Does checking the calculator hurt my credit?

No. This website never contacts a credit bureau or requests a credit report. Your answers are processed locally in your browser.

Do salary, age, or job title affect my FICO Score?

These are not included in the standard FICO credit scoring factors. Lenders may evaluate some financial information separately when making a lending decision.

Where can I see my real credit report?

You can access credit reports from the three nationwide credit reporting companies at AnnualCreditReport.com. Credit reports themselves are different from credit scores.
