What-if scenario analysis

What could happen if I pay this card or collection?

Enter each credit card, try different payment amounts, and compare leaving a collection unpaid versus paying or settling it. The results show how your reported balances and an independent educational score illustration would change.

Important: These are not actual or validated FICO scores, or predictions of how many points your score will change. We use a simplified model and example assumptions for other credit factors. No credit report, login, or personal identity information is requested.

1. Credit cards

Enter reported balances and limits (not necessarily today's live balances). Adjust the payment for each card to see the scenario.

Payments in this example reduce balances, but do not change your credit limits or close accounts. The actual credit report updates when the lender reports.

2. Loans and credit lines

Add auto loans, mortgages, student loans, personal loans, credit-builder loans, HELOCs, or other financing. Enter the original borrowed amount for installment loans, or the credit limit for a HELOC. Then test a payoff amount.

Installment-loan payoff ratios differ from credit-card utilization. Paying off a loan may help, have no immediate effect, or even lower a FICO score in some circumstances; this simulator tracks the balances but does not invent a point increase for loan payments. BNPL reporting can vary.

3. Collection accounts

For each account, choose the reporting type and whether it would be paid or settled to $0. FICO 10/10T generally disregards paid/zero-balance third-party collections, but a real score outcome is not guaranteed.

A first-party collection or separate late-payment history can remain negative even after payment. Check the reporting status on your actual credit reports.

4. Compare scenarios

The illustrative score holds other factors constant using an example profile: eight-year oldest account, four-year average age, one recent inquiry, no late payments, and an installment loan. Use the original calculator separately for a full personal five-factor illustration.

The scenario recalculates as you edit. All calculations happen locally in your browser.

Methodology notes

Credit-card utilization = reported card balance ÷ credit card limit. HELOC balances are displayed separately because reporting and model treatment may vary. Mortgage, auto, student, personal, and other installment borrowing is tracked against the originally borrowed amount. No exact score-point effect is inferred from these payments.

Credit utilization = reported revolving balance ÷ credit limit. The illustrative score uses the five public FICO category weights with independently selected assumptions; its precision is not established. Individual-account utilization and cards reporting balances are shown as educational indicators, but not converted into invented exact FICO point changes.

Sources: myFICO scoring factors · myFICO collections · FICO versions · FICO installment-loan payoffs.

Footnote: older FICO models

FICO versions differ. Older versions may count some paid collections that newer versions disregard. Some lenders continue to use older FICO versions; this site is not a substitute for a score from a credit bureau or lender.
